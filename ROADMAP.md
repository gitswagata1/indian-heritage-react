# Roadmap

## Vision

A rich, accessible digital museum of Indian culture that schools and travellers can rely on.

## Milestones

### Now

- [ ] Add automated tests for the feedback form validation
- [ ] Replace placeholder content with sourced, cited articles and images
- [ ] Persist feedback to a backend or a form service

### Next

- [ ] Dedicated pages for each section (history, festivals, cuisine, art forms)
- [ ] Search and filters by region and period
- [ ] Image galleries with alt text and captions

### Later

- [ ] Multilingual content (Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and more)
- [ ] Interactive map of heritage sites
- [ ] Content management through Markdown or a headless CMS

## Where this can go

| Direction | Idea |
| --------- | ---- |
| **Education** | A free teaching resource for schools, with lesson plans and quizzes. |
| **Publication** | A curated, cited open dataset of festivals and traditional art forms. |
